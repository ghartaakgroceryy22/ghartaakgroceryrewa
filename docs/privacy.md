# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**App Name:** Ghar Tak Grocery  
**Location:** Rewa, Madhya Pradesh, India  

Ghar Tak Grocery values your trust and privacy. This document outlines how your data is handled when you use the Ghar Tak Grocery Android application and online services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Delivery Address & Location:** Used strictly to route grocery deliveries to your address in Rewa.
- **Phone Number / Name:** Used to confirm delivery drop-offs and share order updates.
- **Order History:** Saved locally on your device using encrypted Room database storage.

### 2. How Information is Used
- To dispatch and deliver groceries within 15 minutes.
- To provide order tracking status and delivery receipts.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
